Heritage stays

Stay in the farmhouse the first generation built in the 1920s, kept with its original floors, stone hearth, and windows over the lower pasture. Breakfast comes from the kitchen garden and the farm’s own eggs, and the herd is at the fence by morning.

The day

How it goes

There is no television. There is a woodstove, a shelf of books, the family’s old photographs in the hall, and a porch that looks over the herd.

  1. I

    Arrival

    Come in the afternoon. We show you the house, then walk you down to meet the herd.

  2. II

    Evenings

    A fire in the hearth, supper in the farmhouse kitchen, and the porch when the light goes.

  3. III

    Mornings

    Breakfast is left in the kitchen for you. The herd is usually at the fence before you are up.

  4. IV

    Your days

    Rest, walk the farm roads, or add a trail ride, a milking, or a herd walk. Overnight guests choose dates first.

What to bring

Come ready

  • Boots for the pasture
  • Warm layers in spring and autumn
  • Food for lunch and supper. The kitchen is yours
  • Please leave dogs at home

Questions

Good to know

Has the farmhouse been modernized?

The plumbing, heating, and kitchen are new. The floors, hearth, and windows are as the first generation left them.

Is it suitable for young children?

Yes. There is a cot and a high chair. The stairs are steep, as in most old houses.

Can we join a milking or a herd walk?

Yes. Tell us when you reserve and we will set dates around your stay.

Reserve

Ask for a date
