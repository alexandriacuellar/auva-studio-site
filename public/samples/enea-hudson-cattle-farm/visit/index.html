Questions

Before you come

How do I reserve an experience?

Send the form above or telephone. We confirm dates by email with the address, what to wear, and what to bring.

May I bring children?

Yes, to herd walks, milkings, and stays. Everyone stays on our side of the fence unless we invite you in.

What should we wear?

Boots and clothes you do not mind getting muddy. Each experience page has a short list of what to bring.

Can we just drop by?

Please do not. We are often out in the fields, and the address goes out with your confirmation. Telephone and we will find a time.
