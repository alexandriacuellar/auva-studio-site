Guided herd walks

Walk the paddocks with one of us to meet the herd, the bull, and the year’s calves, and hear how each bloodline came to the farm. The usual first visit for buyers, and for anyone considering a herd of their own.

Season
Year round, Saturdays
Duration
About one hour
Group
Families welcome
Reserve Call (845) 555-0142

The day

How it goes

If you are thinking about buying, start here. You see how the animals are kept, how they move, and how they take to people.

  1. I

    At the gate

    We meet you by the barn and walk out to the paddock the herd is grazing that week.

  2. II

    Cows and calves

    Time among the cows and the year’s calves. Most of them will come over to see you.

  3. III

    The bull

    We stop at the bull’s paddock and talk about his line and his calves, from our side of the fence.

  4. IV

    The herd book

    Back at the barn, we open the herd book to the page of any animal you would like to know more about.

What to bring

Come ready

  • Boots. The paddocks are muddy in spring
  • Your questions, especially if you are thinking about buying
  • Children are welcome. Dogs are not

Questions

Good to know

Can we touch the animals?

Often, yes. Most of the herd is used to people. We will tell you which ones to give room to.

Is the walk suitable for a wheelchair or stroller?

The barn and the first paddock are on level ground. Telephone and we will plan a route that works.

Do I have to be buying?

Not at all. Plenty of people come just to meet the herd.

Reserve

Ask for a date

Tell us a few dates that suit you and how many are coming. We confirm by email with the address, what to wear, and what to bring.
