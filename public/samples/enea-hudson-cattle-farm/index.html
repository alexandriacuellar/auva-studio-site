Our story

Where Highland resilience meets an open gate

For more than a century, our family has worked this Hudson Valley land, keeping a ranching tradition passed down through four generations. At the heart of it is the breed: a hundred years spent refining the bloodlines, strength, and resilience of our Highland cattle.

The bloodlines

Highlands have been the heart of the farm since the 1920s. In 1987 the family added a small registered Angus herd, kept as a separate purebred line and used to breed Highland × Angus crosses. Both herds are closed, so every animal we offer traces back through lines the family has documented.

All animals available How buying works

  • Breeding females

    Open and bred heifers, and proven cows with calf at side, each with its dam’s calving record.

  • Herd bulls

    One or two a year, semen tested, from the Glen Ard, Kinloch, Ben Rua, and Blackwillow lines.

  • Weaned calves

    Highland, Angus, and Highland × Angus calves at six to eight months, halter-started and ready to travel.

  • First-herd guidance

    Fencing, water, shelter, and winter forage, talked through before you buy. Included for every buyer.
