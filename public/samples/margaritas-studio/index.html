Art

Watercolor paintings

I paint at my kitchen table by the window, mostly flowers from the garden, the Long Island Sound and the faces people send me. Every original is one of a kind, signed on the back, and ready to frame.

For a custom portrait, send me two or three photos of your pet, your house or a place that matters to you. I send a small sketch first, so you can tell me what to change before I paint.

Photography

Children’s photography

Children are my favorite people to photograph. No stiff poses: we play, explore and laugh, and I catch the real moments in between. Most sessions are outdoors around Norwalk in the soft light just before sunset, and siblings and parents are always welcome to join in.

You get your edited photos in an online gallery within two weeks, ready to download and print. I also photograph products for makers and small shops, on a clean background or styled.

About

Hi, I’m Margarita

I’ve always made things with my hands: painting at the kitchen table, stitching gifts for friends, and carrying a camera everywhere. Margarita’s Studio brings all of it together in one place.

Every piece is made by me, here in Norwalk, one at a time. I love making things that mark a moment, like a new baby, a wedding, a beloved pet or a favorite beach.

With love, Margarita

Questions

Good to know

How long does a custom piece take?

Most paintings and hoops take two to three weeks. Jewelry usually takes one to two weeks. Need it sooner? Ask, and I will tell you honestly if I can make it.

Do you ship?

Yes, anywhere in the US. Shipping is added at cost. Local pickup in Norwalk is always free.

Where do photo sessions take place?

Outdoors around Norwalk, such as Calf Pasture Beach, Cranbury Park or your own backyard. Golden hour is my favorite, and it’s perfect for little ones.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes, in any amount, for any of the four crafts. They make lovely birthday and holiday gifts.

How do I pay?

Cash, Venmo or card. Custom pieces need a 50% deposit, and the rest is due at pickup or before shipping.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes! I speak English and Spanish, so write to me in whichever you prefer.
