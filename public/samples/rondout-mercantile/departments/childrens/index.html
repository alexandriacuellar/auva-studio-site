Children’s clothing

A small rail of children’s clothes in natural cloth: rompers, smocks, cardigans and a few things for special days. Most of it is made in small workshops in France and England, and some of it in our own workroom.

We can make a christening gown, a ring bearer’s suit or a first jacket to measure, often from the same cloth as a parent’s.
