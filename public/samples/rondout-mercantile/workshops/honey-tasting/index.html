What happens

The tasting

Six honeys, from spring to autumn, from hives within thirty miles of the shop. Two of our beekeepers will be there to talk about where their bees forage and why each jar tastes different.

We serve the honey with bread, cheese and apples from the valley. Your ticket counts toward a jar to take home.
