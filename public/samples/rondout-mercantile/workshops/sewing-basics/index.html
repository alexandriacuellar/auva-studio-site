What happens

The sewing class

A small, unhurried class at the big table in the workroom. We start with how a sewing machine works, then thread it, wind a bobbin and sew straight seams on scrap linen.

By the end of the morning you will have sewn a tote bag in linen from our shelves, and you will know enough to start on a simple pattern at home.

Six people at most, so everyone gets help.

What to bring

  • Nothing. We provide machines, cloth and thread
  • Reading glasses, if you use them
