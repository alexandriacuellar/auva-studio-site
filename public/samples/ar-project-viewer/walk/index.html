How it works

  1. Open the link

    On a Meta Quest, tap “Walk in VR”. On a phone or computer, walk right on this page.

  2. Walk through the house

    In VR, the left stick walks and the right stick turns. Or point at the floor and pull the trigger to jump there.

  3. Try it your way

    The menu on your left wrist switches the finish, turns on night, and takes you room to room. Press Y or B to bring it in front of you. A or X jumps to the next room. With one controller, the menu waits in front of you.

Works on Meta Quest 2, 3, and 3S in the Quest browser. On a computer, drag to look and use WASD or the arrow keys. On a phone, drag to look and tap the floor to walk.
