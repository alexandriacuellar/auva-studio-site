Opens your camera in the browser. No app needed.

Open this page on your phone or Meta Quest 3 to set the house on your table. Scan the code with your phone camera.

How it works

  1. Tap “See it on your table”

    Your camera opens in the browser. Nothing to download.

  2. Point at a table or the floor

    The house follows your phone. Tap to set it down.

  3. Walk around it

    Lift the roof, change the finish, see it at night, or switch to full size outside.

Works on Android phones and Meta Quest 3 with all the buttons. On iPhone, pick the finish and roof first, then the house opens in Apple’s AR view.
